How to play?

Essential steps to become an expert fisher

In this fishing game, you can complete challenges to earn points. These points can be used to purchase better fishing rods, which have better durability to catch more fish.

As you progress, you can continue to upgrade your equipment to catch even rarer and bigger fishes.

To start progressing, go to the shop and purchase the basic rod for free and try catching some fish.

You can complete challenges to buy better rods later on and enlarge your inventory.

You can view the fishes you and your friends caught by going into the backpack.

Eat your fishes by going into the backpack and eating the fishes.

Ran out of durability? Delete your rod and buy a new one!
